paper-rip.m4a: "Paper Ripping", supplied by the project owner (the shredder's discard).
paper-fold.m4a: one fold cut from "Paper Folding", supplied by the project owner (a review card turning over).
clock-tick.m4a: four tick-tocks cut as a loop from "Old Clock Ticking" by universfield (Pixabay, sound 352288), supplied by the project owner (the tick while a session runs).
clock-tense.m4a: one second cut from "Slow Cinematic Clock Ticking Tension 2" by u_mx4xkr2bzy (Pixabay, sound 323078), supplied by the project owner (a session's last ten seconds).
clock-wall.m4a: twelve ticks cut as a loop from "The Mechanical Wall Clock Ticks One Minute" by spinopel (Pixabay, sound 253101), supplied by the project owner (Wall clock, a choice of tick in Settings).
clock-watch.m4a: twelve ticks cut as a loop from "Clock Ticking" by reddog0607 (Pixabay, sound 365218), supplied by the project owner (Pocket watch: small, dry ticks, five a second).
clock-desk.m4a: twelve ticks cut as a loop from "Clock Ticking Sound Effect" by 11325622 (Pixabay, sound 240503), supplied by the project owner (Desk clock: brighter ticks, about four a second).
All three brought to the loudness of clock-tick.m4a (Old clock; Wall clock is the default).
clock-alarm.m4a: three rings cut from "Alarm Clock" by freesound_community (Pixabay, sound 90867), supplied by the project owner (a session's time up).
saber-on.m4a: "Swing3" (Pixabay, sound 94210) into a moment of "Saber Humming.wav" (Pixabay, sound 14651), both by freesound_community, supplied by the project owner (into the sandbox).
saber-off.m4a: a moment of "Saber Humming.wav" played backwards, from the same file (out of the sandbox).
The eight above came with no licence text in the files. Their file names follow Pixabay's download naming, whose Content License allows use in an app without credit; the sources are named here anyway.
page-turn.m4a: the first 0.3s of paper-fold.m4a, faded and brought up to a level you can hear (a piece of writing opened, a note opened in a new pane; played faster and softer when stepping with the arrow keys). The first cut was so quiet it could not be heard.
paper-slide.m4a: 0.28s from the middle of paper-rip.m4a, made mono, its rasp filtered off and faded in and out (read mode opening, the reading queue moving on). A pane closing plays paper-fold.m4a faster and softer.
