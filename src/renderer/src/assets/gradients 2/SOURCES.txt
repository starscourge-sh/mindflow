Gradients: the soft covers a note with no picture of its own wears when
Settings > Appearance > Covers is Gradients and no folder of your own is
chosen (components/haligtree/grain.tsx, GRADIENTS). The app lays a tint of the
note's colour and a film grain over them, so the files themselves stay plain.

Source: the user's own folder of gradients, ~/Documents/img/gradients. Copied
out, resized to 480px on the short side and saved as webp; the originals were
not changed. test-5.jpg and test-6.jpg are the same files as w-1 and w-2, so
only the nine w- files are bundled:
