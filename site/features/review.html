Keep it in your words. Review brings it back.

A gem is a note worth keeping, written your way. Give it the question it answers, and Review asks that question just before you would forget, a little later each time.

How it works

  • One card at a time. Space turns it over; 1 to 4 says how well you knew it. What you could not recall comes back soonest.
  • Cards from inside your notes. Write Capital of Peru :: Lima on any line of any note and it becomes a card, kept in step as you edit the line.
  • More than question and answer. Blank out a few words to fill back in, or add where a gem came from and why it matters, shown after the answer.
  • Help when a card keeps slipping. Miss one a fourth time and Review offers to rewrite it, split it or set it aside.
  • A queue you can finish. Daily limits keep it short, and a reminder at the time you pick says how many cards and about how long. Miss a day and the cards simply wait.
